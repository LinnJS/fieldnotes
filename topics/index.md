# Topics

Each topic is a reading path through the library. A topic opens with a scope statement in the library's own words, reports what the mirrored chapters say with a citation on every paragraph, and ends with a list of where to read. The last column counts the mirrored chapters a topic references. A count of 0 means that no mirrored chapter covers the topic yet and that its books are read at their official sources.
